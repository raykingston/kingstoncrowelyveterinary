Veterinary Surgeon – Kingston Crowley Veterinary

Gerard qualified as a veterinary surgeon in 1991 and has been working in Mallow since then, bringing over 35 years of veterinary experience to the profession.

He has a keen interest in all aspects of veterinary medicine and particularly enjoys the variety that comes with working in mixed veterinary practice. No two days are ever the same, and he finds the diversity of cases, clinical challenges and opportunities to work with both small and large animals especially rewarding.

Having worked in Mallow for over three decades, Gerard greatly appreciates the loyalty and support of his clients over the years and the relationships that have developed along the way. He is also particularly pleased with the team of staff currently working alongside him at Kingston Crowley Veterinary and recognises the importance of having a dedicated and enthusiastic team in providing the best possible care for clients and their animals.

His experience, broad clinical interests and continued enthusiasm for veterinary medicine remain central to his approach to the profession.

Kenneth Crowley

Veterinary Surgeon | Practice Director

Kenneth grew up on a farm surrounded by animals, including cattle, horses, dogs and cats. From a very young age, he dreamed of becoming a veterinary surgeon, inspired by the vets who visited the family farm. He admired their knowledge, skill and the remarkable work they carried out caring for animals.

That childhood ambition developed into a lifelong passion for veterinary medicine. Kenneth has gained extensive experience in mixed veterinary practice and has a particular interest in both farm animal medicine and the care and treatment of companion animals, especially dogs and cats.

As a director of Kingston Crowley Veterinary, Kenneth enjoys the variety that mixed veterinary practice brings, from working closely with farmers to improve herd health and productivity to caring for much-loved family pets. He takes great pride in the relationships he has built with clients over the years and is committed to providing practical, professional and compassionate veterinary care.

Pernille Johanson

Veterinary Surgeon

Originally from Oslo, Norway, Pernille graduated from the University of Veterinary Medicine in Budapest before moving to Ireland in spring 2025. She joined the Kingston Crowley Veterinary team in summer 2026.

Pernille enjoys the variety and challenges of mixed veterinary practice, where no two days are ever the same. She particularly values getting to know her patients and building strong relationships with farmers and pet owners alike.

Outside of work, Pernille enjoys hiking and spending time with her two much-loved collies, who you may well meet at the practice or accompanying her on farm calls!

Veterinary Nurse - Pauline Fitzgerald
Originally from County Limerick, Pauline now lives in North Cork with her son and a lively collection of animals, including four dogs, a blind cat and a turtle.

Pauline qualified as a Veterinary Nurse from University College Dublin (UCD) in 2005 and has worked in the veterinary sector since 1997, gaining extensive experience in both equine and mixed veterinary practices.

Pauline enjoys all aspects of veterinary nursing, with a particular passion for surgery and caring for animals throughout their treatment and recovery.

Outside of work, Pauline has a great love for animals and dedicates much of her spare time to fostering dogs and other animals for various rescue organisations. Having travelled extensively over the years, she also enjoys riding her motorbike and attending motorcycle events whenever she gets the opportunity.

Reception & Veterinary Support - Deirdre Cremin

Deirdre grew up on a dairy farm in Leap, West Cork, surrounded by cows, horses, dogs and cats. Her upbringing fostered a lifelong love of animals and a strong appreciation for farming and rural life.

Deirdre studied Animal Care and Animal Science through the Veterinary Nursing pathway at St. John's Central College in Cork.

Deirdre now lives in Mallow with her family, including her two daughters and son. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her much-loved dogs and horses and is actively involved in pony racing. She also enjoys supporting her children in their sporting activities, particularly with their local club, Kilshannig GAA.

At Kingston Crowley Veterinary, Deirdre is a friendly and familiar face in the office, where she enjoys meeting clients and helping with their enquiries. She also assists the veterinary team with TB testing on farms, combining her practical farming background with her passion for animal care.
