Appointments

Book an appointment

All appointments are booked by phone. Call us on 022 21258 and our team will find a time that suits you and your pet.

Call to book

Give us a ring during opening hours and we'll book you in. We can arrange:

  • Consultations for illness, injury or a check-up
  • Puppy, kitten and booster vaccinations
  • Neutering and planned surgery
  • Post-op checks after surgery
  • Repeat prescriptions (subject to vet approval)
  • Prescription diets and pet food

It helps to have your pet's name, age and a short description of what they need when you call.
