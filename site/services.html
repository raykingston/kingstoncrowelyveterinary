Companion animals

Gentle, thorough care for your pets

Dogs, cats and other small animals get the same patient, unhurried approach, from their first puppy or kitten vaccinations right through to their senior years.

Our vets and veterinary nurse work closely together, so your pet is in experienced hands from the consulting room through surgery and recovery. We'll always explain what we're doing and why, and talk you through your options.

Farm & large animals

Practical support for North Cork farmers

Our vets grew up around farms and understand the realities of farming life. We focus on preventative, practical herd health that protects your animals and your business.

Dairy herd health

Whole-herd planning to keep your dairy cows healthy, productive and performing season after season.

Fertility

Fertility work and advice to help you hit your breeding targets and keep a tight calving pattern.

Mastitis control

Investigation, treatment and prevention strategies to reduce mastitis and improve milk quality.

Preventative medicine

Vaccination and disease-prevention plans built around your farm, because prevention is always better than cure.

TB testing

On-farm TB testing carried out efficiently by our vets with support from our experienced team.
